# Website Identity & Positioning Specification

## Core Strategic Positioning
The website is defined as a **digital archive and active computational node**. It represents a professional space for an AI researcher and systems engineer who operates at the intersection of probabilistic models (Large Language Models) and deterministic logic (formal verification).

## Target Audiences & Desired Understanding
1.  **Academic Counterparts & Thesis Supervisors:** Must recognize rigorous methodology, clear abstracts, and the application of formal proofs to complex clinical reasoning.
2.  **Deep-Tech AI Engineers:** Must validate architectural competence in CLI tooling, containerization, and solver backends—demonstrating expertise beyond standard API wrappers.
3.  **Systems Programmers:** Must appreciate raw performance, headless management, and a commitment to low-level C++ environments.

## Brand Differentiators & Themes
The core competitive advantage is the synthesis of philosophical depth and mechanical, mathematically sound execution. 
*   **Themes:** Deterministic Logic, Verifiable Intelligence, Systems Architecture, Academic Rigor, and Computational Efficiency.

## Communication Benchmarks (The Hybrid Model)
The user experience is designed as a progressive disclosure of technical depth:
*   **Within 10 Seconds:** The visitor reads the identity manifesto and concludes: "This is a rigorous architect building verifiable AI systems and low-level infrastructure."
*   **Within 30 Seconds:** The visitor sees the "Pinned Highlights" (top 2-3 projects) and registers concrete evidence of technical competence.
*   **After Deeper Exploration:** The site reveals an ongoing, chronologically sorted activity feed of notes and commits, showcasing a thinker who engages deeply with complex problems.

## Visual & Editorial Tone
The aesthetic is defined as **Deterministic Brutalism**. The site is stripped of web bloat, relying on data-dense layouts, stark contrast, and typography that mirrors code environments (Serif for human prose, Monospace for machine execution).
*   **Tone of Voice:** Analytical, uncompromising, mechanically raw, and authoritative.

## Anti-Patterns & Constraints
*   **Avoid:** Generic "Hi, I'm a developer" hero sections; smooth/bouncy web animations; marketing-centric language; boasting without evidence (Proof over Promise).
*   **Exclude:** Soft shadows, glassmorphism, generic device mockups, skill progress bars (e.g., "80% Python").

## Post-Graduation Evolution
The architecture of this positioning uses standard taxonomies (`/projects`, `/research`, `/notes`) to ensure longevity. It is designed to seamlessly transition from showcasing undergraduate thesis proposals to hosting published graduate papers or specialized industry contributions without requiring a structural rebrand.
